5. System Grade & Signalling

▾

Grade 1 – Low risk. Little knowledge of alarm systems. Typically domestic/low-value commercial.

Grade 2 – Low-medium risk. Some knowledge. Most domestic/small commercial.

Grade 3 – Medium-high risk. Specialist equipment. Banks, jewellers, high-value.

Grade 4 – High risk. Full substitution attack possible. Extreme-risk premises.

Alarm Transmission System (ATS) categories – BS EN 50136-1

Single path (SP1–SP6) – one transmission path, e.g. IP or radio alone. Higher numbers mean shorter reporting times and closer supervision of the path.

Dual path (DP1–DP4) – two independent paths, e.g. IP with radio backup, or radio with radio. Used where signalling must continue if one path fails.

Bells only – no remote signalling; audible warning devices only.

The category required depends on the system grade and the notification option chosen – see PD 6662:2017 Table 10.
